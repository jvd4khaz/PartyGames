SETUP:
  • Players: 3, 4, or 5 players
  • Deal cards: All 40 cards (36 color + 4 trumps)
    • 3 players: 13, 13, 14 cards (1 card unused)
    • 4 players: 10 cards each
    • 5 players: 8 cards each
  • Commander: Player with King of Spades (leads first trick)
  • Communication Token: Each player needs 1 item (coin, stone, button, etc.)
TASK CARDS:
  • This app automatically generates random task cards when you select a mission
  • Number shown (e.g., "🎴x4") = how many tasks to complete
  • Click "RETRY" to generate new random tasks
  • Manual method (without app): Shuffle all 36 possible cards (A-9 of each suit), draw the required number, place face-up
  • Players take turns selecting tasks (Commander picks first)
  • Each player must take at least 1 task
  • Place order tokens on tasks if mission requires them
COMMUNICATION TOKEN:
  • Use any item: Coin, stone, button, paper clip, etc.
  • Once per mission: Place on ONE card in your hand
  • Position indicates meaning:
    • Top of card: "This is my HIGHEST card of this suit"
    • Middle of card: "This is my ONLY card of this suit"
    • Bottom of card: "This is my LOWEST card of this suit"
  • Place before mission starts (unless Disruption rule applies)
  • Cannot move once placed until next mission
SYMBOLS:
  • ❓ DEAD ZONE: Show card, but NO High/Low/Only indicator
  • ⚡ DISRUPTION: No communication until specified trick
  • ⬇️ COMMANDER'S DECISION: Commander assigns ALL tasks to one player
  • 🔱 COMMANDER'S DISTRIBUTION: Commander assigns tasks one-by-one
  • ⚖️ BALANCE: No player wins 2+ more tricks than another
  • 🎯 TARGET: Specific card/trick requirement
  • 🚫 RESTRICTION: Cannot win with specific cards
  • 🚀 TRUMP SEQUENCE: Trumps must win in order
  • 🔄 SWAP: May rearrange task tokens
  • 🔀 CONFUSION: Special card-swapping rule
ORDER TOKENS:
  • ① ② ③ ④ ⑤: Sequential order (1st, 2nd, 3rd...)
  • Ω: Must be completed LAST
  • > >> >>> >>>>: Relative order (>> after >, etc.)
  • Use any markers: numbered stones, paper tokens, etc.
