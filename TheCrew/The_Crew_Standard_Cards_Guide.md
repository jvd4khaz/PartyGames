# The Crew: Playing with Standard Playing Cards

This guide provides everything needed to play The Crew: The Quest for Planet Nine using a standard 52-card deck (without Jokers), along with simple tokens or stones.

## Component Mapping

### Card Deck Setup

**From Standard Deck:**
- **36 Color Cards**: Use Ace (as 1) through 9 from four suits:
  - **Hearts**
  - **Diamonds**
  - **Clubs**
  - **Spades** (Ace-9)
- **4 Rocket Cards (Trumps)**: Use the high cards from the Spades suit:
  - **10 of Spades** = Rocket 1
  - **Jack of Spades** = Rocket 2
  - **Queen of Spades** = Rocket 3
  - **King of Spades** = Rocket 4 (highest card in the game)

**Card Values:**
- Ace = 1 (lowest)
- 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (increasing order)
- Rocket cards: 10 < Jack < Queen < King

**Removed Cards:**
- Remove all 10, Jack, Queen, and King cards from Hearts, Diamonds, and Clubs.
- Note: Ace-9 of Spades are regular color cards; 10-King of Spades are the Trumps.

### Token System

You'll need simple tokens or stones to represent game components:

#### Communication Tokens (5 needed)
- Use 1 token per player (stone, coin, or button)
- Position it on the card to indicate its meaning:
  - **Top of Card**: "This is my highest card of this suit"
  - **Middle of Card**: "This is my only card of this suit"
  - **Bottom of Card**: "This is my lowest card of this suit"

#### Task Tokens (10 needed)
Create or mark tokens for task order:

**Sequential Order (5 tokens):**
- Token "1", "2", "3", "4", "5" - Tasks must be completed in this exact order.

**Relative Order (5 tokens):**
- Token "Ω" (Omega) - This task must be completed last.
- Token ">", ">>", ">>>", ">>>>" - Tasks must be completed in this relative sequence.

#### Special Mission Symbols
- **Dead Zone (?)**: Communication allowed, but NO High/Only/Low indicators.
- **Disruption (Lightning Bolt)**: Communication forbidden until the start of the trick number shown (e.g., ⚡3).
- **Commander's Decision (Down Arrow)**: Commander assigns ALL tasks for the mission to one single player (not themselves).
- **Commander's Distribution (Three Arrows)**: Commander reveals tasks one by one and decides who receives each individual task.

## Setup Instructions

1. **Prepare the Deck**: Assemble the 40-card deck (36 color cards + 4 trumps).
2. **Assign Commander**: The player with the **King of Spades** (Rocket 4) is the Commander.
3. **Deal Cards**: Deal all 40 cards evenly (3 players: 13/13/14, 4 players: 10 each, 5 players: 8 each).
4. **Draw Tasks**: Draw the number of task cards required by the mission.
5. **Assign Tasks**: Starting with the Commander, players take turns picking tasks until all are assigned.

## Mission Logbook Highlights

### Training (Missions 1-6)
- **M5: Sick Crew Member**: Commander decides who is "sick". That player cannot win ANY tricks.
- **M6: Dead Spot**: Relative order (>, >>). **DEAD ZONE** rules apply (no indicators).

### Launch (Missions 7-12)
- **M9: NAVI Module**: An Ace (any suit) MUST win a trick.
- **M11: Meteorite Field**: Commander picks one player who cannot communicate at all.
- **M12: Passing Meteorites**: After the 1st trick, everyone passes one random card to the right.

### The Long Journey (Missions 13-24)
- **M13: Thrust Test**: Win a trick with EACH Trump card (10, J, Q, K Spades).
- **M16: Suit Damage**: No player may win a trick with a 9-value card.
- **M18: Dust Cloud**: **DISRUPTION (⚡2)** - No communication until trick 2.
- **M20: Radar Repair**: **COMMANDER'S DECISION** - Commander assigns all 2 tasks to one player.
- **M24: Commander's Plan**: **COMMANDER'S DISTRIBUTION** - Commander assigns tasks individually.

### Outer Planets (Missions 25-36)
- **M27: Flux Compensator**: **COMMANDER'S DECISION** - Commander assigns all 3 tasks to one player.
- **M29: Coordination**: **BALANCE** - No player may lead by 2+ tricks. **DEAD ZONE** rules.
- **M32: Confrontation**: **COMMANDER'S DISTRIBUTION** - Commander assigns all 7 tasks individually.
- **M33: EVA Repair**: Selected member wins EXACTLY 1 trick (no Trump).
- **M34: Neptune Shakes**: **BALANCE**. Commander must win first and last trick.

### The Ninth Planet (Missions 37-50)
- **M37: Pluto**: **COMMANDER'S DECISION** - Commander assigns all 4 tasks to one player.
- **M41: Landing Prep**: Selected member wins ONLY 1st and Last trick (no Trump).
- **M43: Wormhole**: **COMMANDER'S DISTRIBUTION** - Commander assigns all 9 tasks individually.
- **M44: Jump Preparation**: **TRUMP SEQUENCE** - Trumps must win in order: 10, then J, then Q, then K.
- **M46: Warning Lights**: Player left of the **Spades 9** owner must win ALL Spades (A-9).
- **M50: Final Approach**: One player wins tricks 1-4. Another wins ONLY the last. Others win all between.
