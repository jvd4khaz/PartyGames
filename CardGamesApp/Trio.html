Trio
Quick Reference
Setup: Use A-K (36 cards). Deal evenly. Arrange hand ascending.
Turn: Reveal 2 cards (low/high from any hand or draw pile). Match? Continue. No match? End turn.
Trio: Collect 3 same rank = 1 trio. 3 trios = Win. Trio of 7s = Instant win.
Players
3-6 players
Deck
Standard 52-card deck
Difficulty
Easy to Medium
Play Time
15-30 minutes
Objective

Be the first player to collect three sets of three cards of the same rank (a "trio"). Alternatively, collect a trio of 7s to win immediately.

Setup
  1. Remove all cards except Aces through Queens (ranks 1-12) from a standard 52-card deck. This leaves 36 cards, with each rank appearing three times.
  2. Shuffle the 36 cards thoroughly.
  3. Deal the cards evenly among all players.
  4. If there are leftover cards after dealing, place them face down in the center of the table as a draw pile.
  5. Each player arranges their hand in ascending order from left to right without changing the order of the cards.
Gameplay
  1. Turn Sequence: On your turn, you may reveal up to two cards:
    • The lowest or highest card from any player's hand (including your own)
    • A face-down card from the draw pile
  2. Matching Cards: If the two revealed cards are of the same rank, you may continue your turn by revealing another card.
  3. Non-Matching Cards: If the revealed cards do not match, return them to their original positions, and your turn ends.
  4. Forming a Trio: If you successfully reveal three cards of the same rank, you collect them as a set and place them face up in front of you.
  5. Next Player: The game continues with the next player in clockwise order.
Winning Conditions
Standard Win: The first player to collect three trios wins the game.
Instant Win: If a player collects a trio of 7s, they win immediately, regardless of how many other trios they have collected.
Variations
Spicy Mode: Instead of collecting three trios, aim to collect two consecutive trios (e.g., 4s and 5s, or 8s and 9s).
Team Play: With four or six players, form teams of two. Teammates can share information and strategize together. The first team to collect the required number of trios wins.
