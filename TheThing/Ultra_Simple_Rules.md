# The Thing - Ultra Simple Card Game

## Components
- Standard deck of playing cards
- 4-8 players

## Setup (30 seconds)
1. Deal 1 card face down to each player (their secret role)
2. **Red card = Human, Black card = Infected**
3. For 4-5 players: 1 Infected. For 6-8 players: 2 Infected
4. Shuffle remaining cards into a deck
5. Place 3 cards face up in the center (the "Base")

## How to Play (2 minutes to learn)

**Goal**: Humans win if the Base has 0 cards. Infected win if the Base has 5+ cards OR if they outnumber Humans.

**Each Round**:
1. **Draw**: Each player draws 1 card
2. **Secret Action**: Everyone simultaneously plays 1 card face-down to center (no one sees what you play)
3. **Reveal**: Flip all cards together. **Red cards = remove that many from Base. Black cards = add that many to Base**
4. **Encounter**: If 2+ players played the same color, they "meet"
   - Each player secretly checks their role card
   - **If you're Infected**: Draw 1 card - **Black = you spread infection** (secretly choose one Human to infect), **Red = no spread**
   - **If you're Human**: Draw 1 card - **Black = you get infected** (if an Infected player drew Black), **Red = safe**
   - After draws, any Infected who drew Black secretly tells their chosen player to switch their role card
   - Return cards to deck
5. **Check Win**: Base = 0 (Humans win) OR Base ≥ 5 OR Infected ≥ Humans (Infected win)

## That's It!

**Key Rules**:
- **All actions are hidden** - you can't tell who played what
- Infected must sometimes play Red cards to blend in
- Encounters spread infection when players "meet"
- Everyone contributes, but you don't know who's helping or hurting

**Strategy**: 
- Humans: Play Red cards to fix Base, but watch for suspicious patterns
- Infected: Play Black cards to sabotage, but mix in Red cards to avoid suspicion
- Trust no one - even "helpful" players might be infected

---

**Play Time**: 10-15 minutes  
**Complexity**: Minimal - perfect for parties
