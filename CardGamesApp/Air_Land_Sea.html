Air, Land, & Sea
Quick Reference
Goal: Win 12 points total (multiple rounds). Win 2 theaters per round OR withdraw.
Setup: Shuffle, deal 6 each. Randomly arrange theaters: ✈️ AIR → 🪖 LAND → ⚓ SEA.
Turn: Play card (face-up or down). Resolve ability. Check winner.
Strength: Higher total wins theater. Face-down = Strength 2.
Players
2 players
Deck
Standard 52-card deck
Difficulty
Medium
Play Time
15-20 minutes
Goal & Scoring
Win: 12 Points
First player to reach 12 points wins (multiple rounds).

Per Round Goal

  • Win 2 theaters
  • OR withdraw

Scoring

  • Higher strength wins - Compare total strength per theater. Higher total wins that theater.
  • Face-down: Strength 2 - Cards played face-down have strength 2 by default.
Withdrawal Scoring

If you withdraw, opponent gets points based on cards left in your hand:

Points 1st Player 2nd Player
2 pts 4-6 cards 5-6 cards
3 pts 2-3 cards 3-4 cards
4 pts 1 card 2 cards
6 pts 0 cards 0-1 cards
Setup & Rules
  1. Shuffle, deal 6 each
  2. Randomly arrange theaters: ✈️ AIR → 🪖 LAND → ⚓ SEA

Turn

  1. Play card (face-up or down)
  2. Resolve ability (if any)
  3. Check winner

Concepts

  • ⚡ Instant - One-time ability
  • 🔄 Ongoing - Continuous effect
  • Adjacent - Left/Right theaters
Theaters

Three theaters are randomly arranged at the start of each game:

✈️
AIR (Blue)
🪖
LAND (Green)
⚓
SEA (Navy)

Players compete to control 2 of the 3 theaters by having higher total strength in each theater.
