Air, Land & Sea
Quick Reference
Theaters: Hearts = Air, Diamonds = Land, Clubs = Sea. Spades = Wild.
Setup: Use 2-10 (27 cards). Deal 6 each. Create 3 theater areas.
Play: Play card to matching theater. Higher value wins theater. Control 2+ theaters = Win battle.
Win: First to 12 Victory Points wins game. Withdraw = opponent gets points.
Players
2 players
Deck
Standard 52-card deck
Difficulty
Medium
Play Time
15-20 minutes
Objective

Be the first player to accumulate 12 Victory Points by winning battles across three theaters of war: Air, Land, and Sea.

Setup
  1. Remove all face cards (Jacks, Queens, Kings) and Aces from a standard 52-card deck. Use only cards 2-10, giving you 27 cards (9 cards per suit).
  2. Assign theaters to suits:
    • Hearts (♥) = Air Theater
    • Diamonds (♦) = Land Theater
    • Clubs (♣) = Sea Theater
    • Spades (♠) = Wild cards (can be played to any theater)
  3. Shuffle the 27 cards thoroughly.
  4. Deal 6 cards to each player face down.
  5. Place the remaining 15 cards face down as a draw pile.
  6. Create three theater areas in the center of the table (Air, Land, Sea).
  7. Each player starts with 0 Victory Points.
Gameplay
  1. Battle Round: Players take turns playing one card from their hand to a theater area. Cards must be played to their matching theater (Hearts to Air, Diamonds to Land, Clubs to Sea). Spades can be played to any theater.
  2. Card Values: The number on the card (2-10) represents its strength. Higher numbers beat lower numbers in the same theater.
  3. Playing Cards: On your turn, play one card face-up to the appropriate theater area. You may also choose to pass instead of playing a card.
  4. Winning a Theater: At the end of a battle round, the player with the highest total value in each theater controls that theater. If tied, neither player controls it.
  5. Winning a Battle: A battle ends when:
    • All cards have been played, OR
    • One player withdraws (chooses to end the battle early)
  6. Battle Resolution: The player who controls 2 or more theaters wins the battle. If each player controls 1 theater, it's a tie.
  7. Withdrawing: At the start of your turn, before playing a card, you may choose to withdraw. If you withdraw, your opponent immediately wins the battle.
Scoring
Battle Victory: Win a battle = 2 Victory Points. Win by opponent withdrawing = 3 Victory Points.
Battle Tie: If the battle ends in a tie, each player receives 1 Victory Point.
Battle Loss: Lose a battle = 0 Victory Points.
Winning the Game

The first player to reach 12 Victory Points wins the game. If both players reach 12 points in the same battle, the player who won that battle is the overall winner.

Advanced Rules (Optional)
Tactical Abilities: For a more complex game, assign special abilities to certain cards:
  • Cards 2-4: Low value, but can be played face-down as a "2" wildcard
  • Cards 5-7: Standard strength
  • Cards 8-10: High value, but opponent may force you to discard one card from hand
Multiple Rounds: After each battle, reshuffle all cards and deal 6 new cards to each player. Continue until someone reaches 12 Victory Points.
