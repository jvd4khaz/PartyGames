🔮 Mysterium

A cooperative mystery game where the Ghost guides Psychics through visions

Game Setup

How to Play

Ghost: Secretly assign each Psychic a suspect, location, and weapon. Give them vision cards (Dixit cards) each round to guide them.

Psychics: Receive vision cards each round. Make guesses about your assigned suspect, location, and weapon based on the clues.

Goal: All Psychics must correctly identify their suspect, location, and weapon within 7 rounds. Then work together to find the true culprit in the final round.

Note: Use physical Dixit cards. Enter the card number/name when giving or receiving clues.
