# The Crew: The Quest for Planet Nine - Rules and Missions Guide

## Game Overview

The Crew is a cooperative trick-taking card game where players work together to complete 50 missions in search of Planet Nine. Players must complete tasks while following strict communication rules, making coordination challenging and strategic.

## Components

### Card Deck (40 cards total)

- **36 Color Cards**: Numbered 1-9 in four suits
  - Yellow suit: cards 1-9
  - Pink suit: cards 1-9
  - Blue suit: cards 1-9
  - Green suit: cards 1-9

- **4 Rocket Cards**: Special trump suit, numbered 1-4
  - Rockets always win tricks when played
  - Highest rocket card wins if multiple rockets are played

### Task Cards

- 36 smaller task cards corresponding to the 36 color cards in the main deck
- Each task card shows a specific card that must be won in a trick

### Communication Tokens

- 5 communication tokens (one per player, up to 5 players)
- Each token has two sides:
  - **Green side (Top)**: Indicates the highest card of that suit
  - **Yellow side (Middle)**: Indicates the only card of that suit
  - **Red side (Bottom)**: Indicates the lowest card of that suit

### Task Tokens

10 task tokens used to indicate task order and dependencies:

**Sequential Order Tokens:**
- **Token "1"**: The task must be fulfilled first
- **Token "2"**: The task must be fulfilled second
- **Token "3"**: The task must be fulfilled third
- **Token "4"**: The task must be fulfilled fourth
- **Token "5"**: The task must be fulfilled fifth

**Relative Order Tokens:**
- **Token "Ω" (Omega)**: The task must be fulfilled last
- **Token ">"**: The task must be fulfilled anytime before ">>"
- **Token ">>"**: The task must be fulfilled anytime after ">"
- **Token ">>>"**: The task must be fulfilled anytime after ">>"
- **Token ">>>>"**: The task must be fulfilled anytime after ">>>"

### Special Rules Symbols

- **Dead Zone (?)**: Communication is allowed, but you **cannot** indicate if it is High, Only, or Low. You just show the card.
- **Disruption (Lightning Bolt)**: Communication is delayed. A number (e.g., ⚡3) means communication is forbidden until the start of that trick.
- **Commander's Decision (Down Arrow)**: The Commander assigns **all** tasks for that mission to **one single player** (and cannot choose themselves).
- **Commander's Distribution (Three Arrows)**: The Commander reveals tasks one by one and decides who receives each individual task.

## Setup

### Initial Setup

1. **Shuffle and Deal Cards**
   - Shuffle the 40 large playing cards thoroughly
   - Deal all cards evenly among players
   - In a 3-player game, one player will have 14 cards and another will have 13 cards (one card remains unplayed)
   - In a 4-player game, each player receives 10 cards
   - In a 5-player game, each player receives 8 cards

2. **Prepare Communication Tokens**
   - Each player places their communication token (green side up) in front of them
   - Each player receives a reminder card

3. **Prepare Task Cards**
   - Shuffle the 36 task cards and place them face down as a deck
   - Keep task tokens within reach

4. **Mission Setup**
   - Place the distress signal token face down nearby
   - Determine the commander (the player with the 4-Rocket / Spades King)
   - Place the commander token in front of the commander

## Gameplay Rules

### Trick-Taking Basics

1. **Leading a Trick**
   - The commander leads the first trick of each mission
   - Subsequent tricks are led by the winner of the previous trick
   - The leader plays any card from their hand face up

2. **Following Suit**
   - Players must follow the suit of the lead card if they have a card of that suit
   - If a player cannot follow suit, they may play any card from their hand
   - Players are not required to play trump (rocket) cards

3. **Winning a Trick**
   - The highest card in the led suit wins the trick
   - If any rocket cards are played, the highest rocket card wins
   - The winner collects all cards from the trick and places them face down
   - The winner leads the next trick

4. **Card Rankings**
   - Within each suit: 9 (highest) > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 > 1 (lowest)
   - Rocket cards: 4 (highest) > 3 > 2 > 1 (lowest)
   - Rockets beat all color cards

### Task Completion

1. **Completing a Task**
   - A task is completed when a player wins a trick containing the playing card that matches their task card
   - The player must be the one who wins the trick
   - If another player wins a trick containing a card matching your task, the mission fails

2. **Task Order**
   - Some missions require tasks to be completed in a specific order
   - Task tokens indicate the required order or dependencies

3. **Mission Success**
   - A mission is successful when all tasks are completed according to the mission requirements

4. **Mission Failure**
   - A mission fails if a player wins a trick containing a card matching another player's task, or if order requirements are violated.

## Notes for Playing with Standard Playing Cards

- **Suits**: Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs, Spades (Colors 1-4)
- **Trumps**: 10, Jack, Queen, King of Spades (Ordered 1-4)
- **Number Cards**: Ace (as 1) through 9 for all four suits (36 cards)
- **Communication Tokens**: 1 token per player. Indicators: High (top), Only (middle), Low (bottom).
- **Commander**: The player with the King of Spades (Rocket 4).
