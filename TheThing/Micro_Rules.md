# The Thing - Micro Version

## Setup (10 seconds)
Deal 1 card face-down to each player: **Red = Human, Black = Infected** (1 Infected for 4-5 players, 2 for 6-8). Place 3 cards face-up in center = the "Base". Shuffle remaining deck.

## Play
**Each Round**:
1. Everyone draws 1 card
2. **Secret Action**: Everyone simultaneously plays 1 card face-down to center
3. **Reveal**: Flip all cards. Count **Red cards** = cards removed from Base. Count **Black cards** = cards added to Base
4. **Encounter**: If 2+ players played same color, they "meet"
   - Each player secretly checks their role card
   - **If you're Infected**: Draw 1 card - **Black = you spread infection** (secretly choose one Human in encounter to infect), **Red = no spread**
   - **If you're Human**: Draw 1 card - **Black = you get infected** (if an Infected player in encounter drew Black), **Red = safe**
   - After draws, any Infected who drew Black secretly tells their chosen player to switch their role card
   - Return cards to deck

**Key**: Everyone plays secretly - you can't tell who sabotaged. Infected must blend in by sometimes playing Red cards.

## Win
**Humans win**: Base reaches 0 cards. **Infected win**: Base reaches 5 cards OR Infected outnumber Humans.

**That's it. Trust no one.**
